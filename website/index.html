FAQ

Good questions.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe the agent in plain words, or start from a template, and Kaho Builder sets up the tools and questions for you. The API is there if your developers want it, but it is optional.

Which languages does the agent speak?

Hindi, English and Hinglish today. You can fix the agent to one of them or let it follow the caller as they switch.

Can it call a whole list of people?

Yes. Upload contacts and run a bulk campaign. Kaho AI respects your calling hours, skips numbers on Do Not Call, and can retry people who did not answer, up to three times and never after a no.

What if the agent says something wrong?

Every change starts as a draft that must pass a test before it goes live, and any published version can be rolled back with one click. You can also read the transcript and hear the recording of every call.

Is my customers' data safe?

Personal details such as phone numbers, e-mail addresses and UPI ids are masked before transcripts are stored. Access is controlled by roles, and an audit log records who changed what.

Can the agent use my own systems?

Yes. Give an agent a tool that calls your API, upload documents for it to answer from, and receive a signed webhook with the summary and answers after every call.
